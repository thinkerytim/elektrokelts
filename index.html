The show

All the Irish and Scottish songs you love, cranked out on all the instruments. Guitar, fiddle, mandolin, banjo, bass, some rocking drums, and the occasional accordion. Seven of us, in kilts, playing fast.

What you get

A full night of pub anthems, rebel songs, reels, and sing-alongs with the energy turned all the way up. Drinking songs people already know the words to, old tunes played like punk songs, and no breaks in the momentum.

Where we play

Pubs, beer halls, summer concert series, renaissance faires, off-road festivals, and St. Patrick's weekends across Spokane, Coeur d'Alene, Sandpoint, Newport, and Bonners Ferry. We travel.

Seven on stage

Vocals, two guitars, bass, fiddle, mandolin, banjo, and drums, with bodhrán and accordion when the song calls for it. We bring a big sound and we fit on a small stage.
